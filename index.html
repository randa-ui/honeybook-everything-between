Concept

Be there for the moment.
Everything between is handled.

A wedding photographer is hired for two moments: the first look and the last dance. The film opens there, close on the eyes, the ring, the kiss. That is the job.

Then it widens. Between those two moments sits the whole business: the inquiry that lands at two in the morning, the contract waiting for a signature, the invoice that has to be paid. In the film these appear as paper cards that orbit the photographer's moments and get handled one by one, without the photographer ever putting the camera down.

The visual language is borrowed from the photographer's own desk: polaroids, a camera, a bouquet, a cake topper, laid out on warm off-white paper, with a single accent colour marking every moment the product steps in.

Storyboard

Fifteen beats, two moments, one business in between.

Grey-box frames in script order, each captioned with the line it carries.

  1. 01

    “As a wedding photographer…” The camera sits between the two words that define the job.

  2. 02

    “…your job is to be there for the moment.” A single polaroid drops in.

  3. 03

    The moments multiply. Three polaroids, three sizes, the day as a photographer sees it.

  4. 04

    “The first look.” Two frames, two eyes, the first of the two moments.

  5. 05

    “The last dance.” Two cards lean into each other for the kiss.

  6. 06

    “Everything else happens between them.” The business orbits the moments as a ring of cards.

  7. 07

    “The inquiry. Answered before you're even awake.” The first card lands: a new inquiry.

  8. 08

    “The contract…” The document slides into frame.

  9. 09

    “…signed from the couple's couch…” The contract opens on the couple's laptop.

  10. 10

    “…in one click.” A signature draws itself across the screen.

  11. 11

    “The invoice. Paid on time, every time.” The invoice, as the photographer sees it.

  12. 12

    “…handles your business…” The project view, everything in one place.

  13. 13

    “…so you can focus on the right moment.” The screen pulls back and rejoins the flow of cards.

  14. 14

    The cards settle. Nothing left on the desk.

  15. 15

    “Everything between.” Logo and tagline.
